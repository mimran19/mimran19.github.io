The Believer Reset

Reminders for the seasons that don't make sense yet.

Short reflections for Muslim women sitting in unanswered duas, closed doors, and the long quiet in between.

What this is

The Believer Reset publishes short video reminders grounded in Qur'an and the duas of the prophets. Each one takes a single hard feeling — waiting, exhaustion, a door that shut — and sits with it honestly rather than rushing past it.

No lectures, no promises that everything resolves on your timeline. Just the reminder that the waiting is not evidence of abandonment.
